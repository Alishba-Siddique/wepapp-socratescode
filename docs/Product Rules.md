# Product Rules
[[Home]] - [[Build Plan]]

## Who this is for
Developers building programming fundamentals, independent problem-solving skills, and interview confidence. Teams are an audience; team administration and shared reporting are not implemented yet.

## Learning contract
1. **Predict:** commit to a prediction before seeing a result.
2. **Run:** inspect a deterministic trace, one step at a time.
3. **Investigate:** explain a relevant change in state; wrong answers receive a guiding question.
4. **Modify:** change a supported input and predict how the result changes.
5. **Make:** apply the idea to a new target and record a reflection.

Wrong predictions are learning opportunities. Do not lock someone out for being wrong. Completion must require the actual stage activities; tab clicks alone do not complete a lesson.

## Honesty
- Label the first increment as a guest workspace with guided exercises.
- Browser progress is local, not a signed-in account.
- Predetermined traces are not a general-purpose Python runtime.
- Prepared guiding questions are not a connected AI service.
- Do not fabricate rankings, achievements, customers, or proficiency scores.
- A saved reflection is not automatically proof of semantic understanding.

## MVP acceptance
A learner can open a lesson, predict, step through execution, investigate, modify an input, apply a new target, and return to a dashboard that reflects completed work.
